## Changes from the original (Chris Ivarson, Nov 2026)

- Half-round "alarm clock" top, concentric with the screen; screen and window moved up 11.5 mm
- Screw posts for the Adafruit 1.28" round display (tab down, M2 × 4 mm self-tapping)
- Rails on the inside left wall for the Adafruit SEN6x breakout
- More room for the ESP32-C3 SuperMini (wider and deeper slots, squared corner for solder joints)
- Wiring channel at the top; upper side vents carried through the curved wall
- Three alternative front faces

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `SEN6X_back_v4_clock` | Back half (holds the SEN66, breakout and USB-C opening) |
| `SEN6X_front_v4_clock` | Front half, plain |
| `SEN6X_front_v4_A_grille` | Front half with an 8-slot clock-radio grille (through-slots, extra airflow) |
| `SEN6X_front_v4_B_sunburst` | Front half with engraved art-deco rays |
| `SEN6X_front_v4_D_short_grille` | Front half with a 4-slot grille |

Print one front and the back. Fronts print face-down without supports.
